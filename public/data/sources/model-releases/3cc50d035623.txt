Introduction
We are excited to open-source
Qwen-Image-2.1
, a unified text-to-image generation and image editing model in the Qwen family. With just
7B parameters in its visual generation component
(32 Single-Stream DiT layers), Qwen-Image-2.1 balances generation quality, inference efficiency, and versatility.
Four key improvements define this release:
Compact and Efficient
— A lightweight architecture with mixed-granularity attention and prefix KV cache reuse delivers strong image quality at low computational cost.
Native Transparency, Unified Creation and Editing
— Generate regular or transparent (RGBA) images from text, edit transparent layers, and extract subjects from photographs—all in one model.
Versatile Editing
— Support up to
10 reference images
, specify local edits via circles, painted annotations, or separate masks, and preserve identity for people and products.
Realistic Textures and Refined Aesthetics
— Improved typography, portrait lighting, and fine details for more visually compelling results.
For more details, see the
GitHub repo
and
Blog
.
